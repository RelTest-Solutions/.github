# RelTest

**Reliability engineering, lifetime analysis, technical assurance and professional education.**

RelTest combines scientific methods, engineering practice and digital tools to support robust development decisions and reliable products.

## What we do

### RelTest Solutions

We support companies throughout the development and assurance of reliable technical products and systems.

Our work covers areas such as:

- Reliability Engineering & Reliability Management
- Reliability Planning, Testing & Verification
- Lifetime & Field Data Analysis
- Design of Experiments (DoE)
- Risk & Weakness Analysis
- Engineering Software & Data Analysis

### RelTest Education

We make Reliability Engineering and Design of Experiments accessible through practical and flexible professional education.

Our formats include:

- E-Learning and digital learning environments
- Reliability Engineering programs
- Design of Experiments training
- Practical exercises and industrial case studies
- On-site and in-house training

## Software & Digital Tools

This GitHub organization is the development space for software and digital tools at RelTest.

We build applications and technical resources that support our work in engineering and education, including:

- reliability and lifetime analysis
- statistical engineering methods
- test planning and evaluation
- engineering workflows and automation
- educational software and learning platforms
- tools for creating and managing technical training content

Some repositories support our own engineering and educational infrastructure, while others may provide reusable tools, examples or resources for the wider reliability engineering community.

## Engineering meets Software

Modern Reliability Engineering increasingly depends on reproducible analysis, structured data, automation and well-designed software.

Our development work therefore connects:

**Reliability Engineering · Statistics · Software Engineering · Education**
